Appendix · 08

Resources & Reproducibility

Deliberately tiered — released openly vs. not initially exposed — stated out loud rather than left for a reviewer to wonder about.

Paper (PDF) Supplementary Material Reference Implementation

Released openly

Sufficient to understand and substantially reproduce the approach on ordinary cases.

  • Agent instructions — resources/prompts/diagram_transmuter.md, layout_parser.md, semantic_parser.md, animation_planner.md, narration_drafter.md, animation_designer.md, and the three critics under resources/prompts/critics/
  • Configuration — resources/schemas/, resources/configs/animation_styles/, resources/configs/evaluation/
  • Representative intermediate and end-to-end outputs
  • Benchmark metadata and evaluation subset, as appropriate
  • A reference execution path sufficient for standard examples — resources/reference_implementation/, explicitly labelled reference implementation, not the full experimental system

Not initially exposed

No need for the review artifact to expose the entire production system.

  • Production orchestration and retry/fallback policies
  • Difficult-case recovery heuristics
  • Internal prompt-routing logic
  • Batching / caching infrastructure and the deployment stack
  • Optimisation specific to pathological diagrams, and other internal utilities

The released path is functional for ordinary cases; the paper documents the conceptual method required for full reproducibility.
