Rationale for a reference-free evaluation design

A diagram can be animated in multiple valid ways

Insight

The Feature Learning Sub-network branches into two parallel paths: Bilinear Pooling and the Spectral Averaging Layer. Since either branch can be explored first, multiple animation sequences are valid. For instance, AnimateBench presents Bilinear Pooling first, whereas a baseline may explore the Spectral Averaging Layer first. Although both sequences are valid, direct comparison against the reference would penalize the latter for a difference in ordering alone. Therefore, our evaluation metrics do not use the reference narration as ground truth; instead, they assess the intrinsic quality of each generated narrative.
